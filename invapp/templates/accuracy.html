Book value to physical value

The bridge from what the system said the network was worth a quarter ago to what the count says it is worth now, one step per cause.

Root cause of variance

Only records outside tolerance. Mis-picks and receiving errors dominate most real variance registers; theft is usually the smallest line on them.

Accuracy by node

Record accuracy weights a bin of cables the same as a bin of tablets; value accuracy does not, and is the one finance quotes.

Count coverage

Coverage is the leading indicator that accuracy is the lagging one for. A node reporting 99% on eleven counts is not accurate; it is unmeasured.

What to do about all of it

Every finding on the other five pages, in one list, ranked by money. The dollar column is not comparable across verbs — a reorder’s impact is contribution at risk, a liquidation’s is book value tied up — so ranking within a verb is the ordering that means something.
