Which model won, and how often

Seven methods compete per SKU on a rolling-origin backtest, scored on mean absolute scaled error. A four-week moving average winning on a chunk of the catalogue is the expected result on real demand, not a failure of the exercise.

Where the forecast leans

Bias is signed error over actual: positive means over-forecast. Weeks are shown rather than SKUs because bias that shows up on the same weeks for everything is a calendar problem, and bias scattered across SKUs is a model problem.
